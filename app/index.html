Help / controls

Waypoints: click or drag a visible point; right-click toggles its AI Turbo marker.

Foreground / Surface: choose a drawing tool, paint value and brush. Right-click uses the secondary paint action.

Recovery: rotate arrows with left/right mouse; Grab group supports multi-cell selection.

Fixed viewport: use the 640×512 viewport button with higher zoom, then move around with the trackpad or scrollbars.

Race: drag visible race/pit anchors or edit their fields. MiniMap and Map have their own edit modes.
